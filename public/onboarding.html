Step 2 of 5

Join your Slack channel.

Check your email for an invite from Slack to join Imperial Growth. Click Accept invitation and you’re in your private channel with our team.

No Slack yet? It’s free. The invite walks you through making an account. Stuck? Watch the 30-second video.

Prefer to read the steps?
  1. Open the email from Slack that says Imperial Growth invited you, then click Accept invitation.
  2. New to Slack? Enter your email and click Continue.
  3. Slack emails you a 6-digit code. Type it in.
  4. Name your Slack after your business. It’s free.
  5. Click Accept to join your channel. Then get the Slack app on your phone and turn on notifications.

No email from Slack? Check your spam folder, or message us and we’ll resend it.

Step 4 of 5

Give us access to your ad account.

Watch this short video and follow along. Your ad account stays yours. You’re just letting us run it.

Already did this with us on the call? Skip ahead. Stuck? No stress, save it for our call and we’ll do it together.

Prefer to read the steps?
  1. On a computer, open business.facebook.com/settings and pick your business.
  2. In the menu, go to Users → Partners, click Add, then Give a partner access to your assets.
  3. Enter our business ID: Business ID coming
  4. Pick your ad account and turn on full control. Add your Facebook Page and dataset (pixel) too, if you have them.
  5. Click Assign assets. That’s it. We’ll confirm in Slack.

No Meta business account yet? Go to business.facebook.com, click Create a business portfolio, then in settings go to Accounts → Ad accounts → Add → Create a new ad account. Then do the steps above.

Don’t see Partners? You need to be an admin of your business portfolio. Ask whoever set it up, or save it for our call.
